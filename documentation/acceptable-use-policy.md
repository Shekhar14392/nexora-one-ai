# Acceptable Use Policy

Prohibited on NEXORA AI:
- Spam, unsolicited bulk messaging, or sending to lists without permission.
- Collecting personal information through unauthorized methods, scraping in violation of terms.
- Content that is unlawful, hateful, harassing, deceptive, or infringes others' rights.
- Circumventing platform permissions, social-platform API restrictions, or license verification.
- Financial fraud, money laundering, or sale of prohibited goods or services.
- Attempting to gain access to other tenants' data; tenant isolation is enforced and monitored.

Enforcement: warning, feature restriction, account suspension, and license suspension.
