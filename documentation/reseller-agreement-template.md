Between: <COMPANY NAME> ("Licensor") and <RESELLER> ("Reseller"). Date: <DATE>

   <TERRITORY or "worldwide">.
2. Reseller pricing. Licensor grants Reseller a discount of <X>% on list prices. Reseller sets
   resale prices independently and bears its own taxes.
3. License distribution. Reseller requests licenses through the reseller account. Each license is
   bound to one customer domain and tracked in the platform.
4. White-label. Where agreed, the customer-facing branding is Reseller's; the underlying software
   remains Licensor's intellectual property.
5. Support. First-line support is Reseller's responsibility; platform-level issues go to Licensor.
6. Payment. Invoices are due <NET 15>. Continued reseller rights require current payment standing.
7. Term. <12 months>, auto-renewing; either party may end it with 30 days' notice. Existing
   customer licenses remain valid per their terms.
8. Compliance. Reseller complies with the acceptable-use policy and applicable law.
